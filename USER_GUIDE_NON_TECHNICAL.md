# Vessel Agent System - Captain's Quick Start Guide

**A Simple Guide for Fishing Boat Captains**

## Welcome Aboard!

If you're reading this, you're probably a captain or boat owner who wants to understand what this "vessel-agent system" does without getting bogged down in technical jargon. Let's keep it practical.

---

## What Is This Thing?

Think of this system as a **black box for your boat** that records everything important during your fishing trips and helps you make better decisions.

**What it does:**
- Records what your fish finder (sounder) sees
- Records where your boat is (GPS)
- Saves all this information so you can look at it later
- Helps you find patterns in where the fish are

**What it doesn't do:**
- It doesn't control your boat
- It doesn't replace your judgment
- It doesn't require you to be a computer expert

---

## Why Would You Want This?

### The Problem

You know how it is:

1. **You find a great spot** one day, catch fish like crazy
2. **Next time you go there**... nothing. Why?
   - Was it the time of day?
   - The tide?
   - The water temperature?
   - Just luck?

3. **You try to remember** what worked last season... but details get fuzzy

### The Solution

This system **remembers everything** so you don't have to:

- **Where you were** (exact location)
- **What you saw** (fish finder marks)
- **When you were there** (time and date)
- **What you caught** (your logbook entries)

Then it helps you **find patterns**:
- "Last year at this spot, chinook were thick at 40-50 feet deep in July mornings"
- "This area produces well on flood tides but not ebb"
- "Water temperature was 52°F when we hit the big school"

## How It Works (In Plain English)

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                               │
│    YOUR FISH FINDER    →    THE BLACK BOX    →    COMPUTER │
│      (Furuno)                (Vessel Agent)       (Laptop)     │
│                                                               │
│      Shows you              Records it          Shows you      │
│      fish marks            and saves           patterns       │
│                                                               │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Step 1: Your Fish Finder (Furuno Sounder)**
- This is what you already have
- It shows you fish marks on the screen
- The system taps into this information

**Step 2: The Black Box (Vessel Agent)**
- Small computer program running in the background
- Records what your fish finder sees
- Also records where your boat is (GPS)
- Saves everything to the computer's hard drive

**Step 3: Viewing on Computer**
- You can look at the data later
- See patterns in where fish are
- Compare today to last year
- Plan your next trip

## What Do You Need?

### Hardware (Equipment You Already Have)

**Required:**
- ✅ Fish finder/sounder (Furuno FCV series is standard)
- ✅ GPS (built into most sounders or separate)
- ✅ Laptop computer (Windows or Mac)

**Optional but Nice:**
- Internet connection (for updates, not required for operation)
- Backup drive (to save data)

### Software

**The system does all the technical stuff. You just need to:**

1. Install the program (like installing any other application)
2. Click "Start" when you want to record
3. Click "Stop" when you're done

That's it.

## Using the System: A Day in the Life

### Before the Trip

**5 minutes before you leave:**

1. Turn on your laptop
2. Open the Vessel Agent program
3. Click "Start Recording"
4. Go about your normal pre-trip routine

The system now runs quietly in the background, recording everything.

### During the Trip

**You don't need to do anything!**

The system is quietly:
- Recording what your fish finder sees
- Recording where you are
- Saving all this to the computer

You focus on fishing. The system focuses on remembering.

### After the Trip

**Back at the dock:**

1. Click "Stop Recording"
2. The system saves everything
3. You can now look at what you recorded

## Looking at Your Data

### The Simple View

When you open the system to look at your data, you'll see:

**Where You Were:**
- A map showing your track (where you drove)
- Where you stopped to fish
- How long you spent at each spot

**What You Saw:**
- Fish finder marks (like looking at a history of your screen)
- Bottom depth
- Water temperature (if your sounder shows it)

**What You Caught:**
- Your logbook entries
- What you caught, when, where

### Finding Patterns

The system helps you answer questions like:

"**Where were the fish last Tuesday?**"
- Click on last Tuesday
- See exactly where you were
- See what your fish finder showed

"**When is this spot most productive?**"
- Click on the spot
- See all times you've been there
- Compare good days vs bad days

"**What depth were they at?**"
- Look at the fish finder history
- See what depth the marks were at
- Compare to what you caught

## Common Questions

### "Will this slow down my fish finder?"

**No.** The system just listens to what your fish finder is already sending out. It doesn't interfere with normal operation.

### "Do I need to understand computers?"

**Not really.** If you can:
- Turn on a computer
- Open a program
- Click "Start" and "Stop"

You're good to go.

### "What if something goes wrong?"

**The system is designed to be robust:**
- If it crashes, it restarts automatically
- Your data is saved constantly (nothing lost)
- If there's an error, it tells you in plain English

### "Can I use my existing fish finder?"

**Yes**, if it's a Furuno FCV series (FCV-295, FCV-585, FCV-600, etc.). Other brands may work but need testing.

### "What about my old fishing logs?"

**You can add them!** The system can import:
- Your handwritten notes (type them in)
- Old paper logbooks
- Photos of your catch

This builds a complete history of your fishing knowledge.

## Getting Started: The 15-Minute Setup

### Step 1: Install (5 minutes)

1. Put the USB drive in your computer
2. Run the installer
3. Click "Next" a few times
4. Done!

### Step 2: Configure (5 minutes)

1. Tell the system your boat name
2. Tell it which USB port your fish finder uses
3. Tell it where to save data

### Step 3: Test (5 minutes)

1. Turn on your fish finder
2. Click "Start" in the system
3. Watch for 5 minutes
4. Click "Stop"
5. Look at what it recorded

That's it. You're ready to fish.

## What Makes This Different?

### Unlike Traditional Logbooks

**Vessel Agent:**

- Digital, never gets lost

- Makes comparisons automatic

## Real-World Examples

### Example 1: The Secret Spot

**Captain's Story:**
"I had this spot near Point Arden that produced well a few times, but I couldn't figure out WHY. Sometimes it was great, sometimes nothing."

**With Vessel Agent:**
- Looked at all times fished there
- Noticed pattern: produced best on flood tide, 2 hours after high tide, in July mornings

- **Result: Better catches, less wasted time**

### Example 2: Depth Pattern

**Captain's Story:**
"Thought the fish were always on the bottom, but sometimes we'd catch them mid-water."

**With Vessel Agent:**
- Reviewed fish finder history
- Saw pattern: fish suspended at 40-50 feet when bottom temp was 52°F
- Saw they were on bottom when temp was 48°F
- **Result: Adjusted presentation, better hookup ratio**

### Example 3: Seasonal Migration

**Captain's Story:**
"Every year the chinook seem to show up at different times."

**With Vessel Agent:**
- Compared last 3 years data
- Saw pattern: fish arrive when water temp hits 50°F, not specific calendar date
- **Result: Ready when fish arrive, not guessing by date**
